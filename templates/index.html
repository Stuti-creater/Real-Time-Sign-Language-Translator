How to use

  1. Click Start Camera and allow the camera if asked.
  2. Place your hand 30–60 cm from the camera with your palm facing it.
  3. Keep the whole hand inside the frame, with a plain background and good light.
  4. Hold a sign steady for about 1 second. The progress bar fills, then the letter is typed.
  5. To type the same letter twice, show a different sign in between.
  6. Use Space, Delete, Clear, Copy and Speak to edit and share your text.

Tips for best results

  • Use one hand at a time.
  • Face a window or lamp so your hand is well lit.
  • Move slowly between signs.
  • If a sign shows Gesture not recognized, adjust finger positions to match the guide.

About this project

This college project translates a small vocabulary of static hand signs (inspired by the American Sign Language alphabet) into English text in real time. OpenCV reads the webcam, MediaPipe finds 21 landmarks on the hand, and a rule-based classifier checks which fingers are straight or curled to decide the sign. Flask connects the Python backend to this web page.

Honest limitations: only the gestures listed above are recognized; letters that need motion (J, Z) and other signs are not supported; accuracy depends on lighting, camera angle and hand shape. Anything else is shown as “Gesture not recognized”.
